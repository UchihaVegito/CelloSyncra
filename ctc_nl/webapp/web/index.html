What happens

1 · FindCellpose-SAM (one model for 2D and 3D) draws every cell.
2 · TrackEach cell is followed over time, including when it splits.
3 · AskQwen2.5-72B answers your questions from the measurements.

The assistant never guesses numbers: it calls measurement tools on the cell table, and every answer is drawn on the image so you can check it. Everything runs on the university GPU; images never leave the cluster.
